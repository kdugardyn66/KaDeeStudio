# Crafting App icons

Each Crafting App is shown with that app's own icon. These 128 px PNGs are copied unmodified from
[storytold/craft-launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher) `assets/app-icons/` (commit 48b1a04),
which copies them unmodified from each app's repository. Their designs belong to the ArtCraft Team, Tangency Labs
and each app's contributors, and they are licensed like those apps: MIT OR Apache-2.0. SolidCraft's license
texts are next to its icon. Refresh them from the source; never redraw them.
